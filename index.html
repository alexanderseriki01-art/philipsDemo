Common questions.

Everything teams ask before bringing a cohort onto the platform.

Need something specific? training@phillipsconsulting.net
How are payments verified?

We support Paystack and Flutterwave for instant confirmation, plus manual bank-transfer verification by our finance team, usually within 24 to 48 hours. Once confirmed, materials and the participant seat unlock automatically.

Can we enrol a corporate cohort?

Yes. Organisations can register multiple participants under one account and track attendance, payments, and completion for the whole cohort from a single admin dashboard.

Are the certificates verifiable?

Every certificate carries a unique code and a tamper-evident QR. Anyone can confirm its authenticity on the public verification page, with no login required.

Do you offer hybrid and virtual delivery?

Many programmes run in-person, virtually over Zoom, or as a hybrid of both. The delivery mode and venue are listed on each programme card.

What if a participant misses a session?

QR check-in records attendance per session. Late and absence rules are configurable and gate certification automatically, so completion always reflects real participation.

Ready to digitise your next programme?

Bring your cohorts onto one platform that automates the busy work, so your team focuses on delivery.
